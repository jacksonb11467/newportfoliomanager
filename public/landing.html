👋 Welcome to Portfolio Manager!

Let's take a quick tour of what you can do

1

Start by Adding Your Holdings

Head to Manage Holdings to add your stocks. Search for any ticker (US or ASX) and set your quantities. This is the foundation of everything else!

2

Stay Informed with News

News by Holding gives you the latest articles and AI-powered summaries for each stock. No more manual research - Claude reads the news for you and highlights what matters.

3

Monitor Your Portfolio

The Dashboard shows price movements, charts, and trends at a glance. Perfect for your daily check-in to see how things are performing.

4

Get Daily Intelligence

Daily Digest provides an executive summary of your portfolio - top movers, key news, and action items. Like having your own analyst.

5

Understand Your Risk

Risk & Analysis shows sector concentration, diversification metrics, and alerts you to potential issues. Know where you're exposed.

6

Automate Updates

In Settings, schedule automatic updates and email digests. Set it once and get daily or weekly reports delivered to your inbox.
